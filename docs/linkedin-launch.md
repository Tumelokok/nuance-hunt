# LinkedIn launch draft

I’m starting an open-source project called **Nuance Hunt**. The idea is simple: AI can sound fluent while still missing the meaning, context, or cultural nuance of a prompt in another language.

Nuance Hunt is a small tool for comparing AI answers across languages and recording what a fluent reviewer notices. I’m beginning with a local prototype and want to shape the first pilot with people who know the language and context firsthand.

The goal is to make these language blind spots easier to spot, discuss, and reproduce—not to claim one score can summarize a language or a community.

If you work with multilingual AI or speak a language that deserves better evaluation, I’d love to hear what kinds of real-world tasks we should test first.
