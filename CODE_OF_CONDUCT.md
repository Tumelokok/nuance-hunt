# Community code of conduct

We want people with different languages, dialects, backgrounds, and levels of technical experience to be able to participate.

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and evidence without dismissing a person's language or identity. Do not claim to speak for an entire language community. When reviewing language, state the variety and context you can judge, and make room for other speakers to disagree.

Harassment, slurs, threats, doxxing, and targeted abuse are not acceptable. Do not post private conversations or identifying information without consent. Project maintainers may remove content or limit participation to protect community members and data contributors.

If you experience or witness a problem, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository's security or contact channel once it is published.
